# Live data audit review of pinellas-r0

Work dir: `(session work dir, not committed) ` (scripts `s1_claims.py` … `s7_viewer.py`, outputs `*_out.txt`, `claims.csv`, `s5_scores.csv`, `s5_scored.parquet`, `s4_cells_*.csv`). Every number below is from those runs against `data/flood_v1/assemble/buildings_12103.parquet` (sha256 `ecea7173…d6a93b7`, verified) and its sibling files.

## Verdict

The table is internally sound: 79 of 89 numeric claims in the committed `assemble_12103.json`, `check_12103.txt`, `06-layer-schema-v1.md` and handoff §8–§10 reproduce exactly; every arithmetic rule (bands, `ffe = ground + ffh`, record `ffh = ffe − certificate LAG`, `floor_minus_bfe`, `bfe_call`, `bfe_call_basis`, zone shares, `raised_flag`, null reasons) holds on all 372,764 rows with zero violations, and the viewer JSON carries the table's values byte-for-byte. The headline problem is the accuracy claim, not the arithmetic: scored fresh against 1,267 Pinellas County certificates that r0 never saw (and that are mostly post-1992 elevated houses), the table's modeled floor has MAE 2.21 ft, 90 %-band coverage 0.883, BFE side 0.863 and decided-call accuracy 0.950, against the published gate of 1.039 / 0.918 / 0.898 / 0.983; for elevated houses the model does not flag, the band covers only 54 %. The second problem is `ground_ft`: for 291 waterfront buildings it is a DEM constant (−2.0 ft NAVD88) or water, published as an "observed" value with 0.33 ft precision, and 73 modeled floors (32 "below" calls) are built on it. Fit to show a floodplain manager only with the county-certificate score and its population caveat printed next to the FDEM gate, and with the water-ground rows nulled or flagged. Before the next release: (1) publish the county-label score and drop the unqualified "90 % band" promise for unflagged elevated houses; (2) mask water / DEM-constant cells out of the ground ring; (3) fix the stale §8 counts and the hash typo in the handoff.

## Findings

### 1. [blocking] The published accuracy does not describe the modeled floors on an independent certificate set
- **What:** handoff §3/§8/§10 and the parquet metadata `gate` (MAE 1.039, coverage 0.918, BFE side 0.898, decided correct 0.983) are FDEM held-out numbers. The county layer (`labels_pinellas_12103.parquet`, 5,247 matched; 5,019 native NAVD88) was never used in r0 (`train.py` reads only `train/labels_12103.parquet`; r1b doc confirms r0 untouched). 1,416 county certificates sit on buildings with no FDEM certificate; 1,310 of those rows are `ffh_class = modeled`.
- **Evidence** (`s5_county.py`, `s5_out.txt`; error = table `ffe_ft` − county first living floor; train.py's lidar label screen removed 43 of 1,310 → 1,267):

- **Fix:** add the county-certificate score (this table, by diagram group) to `06-layer-schema-v1.md`, the `spatia_flood` parquet metadata `gate`, the spatia-data sidecar and `county.json.held_out_accuracy`; state the FDEM gate's population; until r1 widens elevated bands, print "coverage not guaranteed for unflagged diagram 5–9 houses" on the card.

### 2. [major] `ground_ft` is water or a DEM constant for waterfront buildings, and modeled floors are built on it
- **What:** `ground_ft` (= `g_lag`, the ring minimum of the 1 m DEM) is published as `ground_class = observed`, precision 0.328 ft, with no null reason, on cells that are not ground.
- **Evidence** (`s3b_out.txt`): 244 rows have `ground_ft` exactly −1.999865 (the most frequent value in the column); 291 rows < −1.99 ft; 770 rows < 0 ft NAVD88 (below mean sea level). For sentinel rows the whole DEM window is −2.0 (e.g. `b85d82a4…`: g_p10 = g_med = g_inside = g_far = −2.00, 695 points, VE, BFE 12, modeled ffe 2.24, call too_close). 73 sentinel rows are modeled (32 below / 45 too_close / 1 above); the four most extreme `floor_minus_bfe_ft` (< −10 ft) are all sentinel rows (ground −2.0, VE, BFE 13–14, call below). For the "neg" group (479 rows, −1.99 < ground < 0) the ring median sits 5.9 ft above the ring minimum (water in the ring); their below share is 0.526 vs 0.113 county-wide. 5,837 modeled buildings have `g_inside − g_lag > 3 ft`. County certificates on `ground_ft < 0` rows: n 8, MAE 6.59, median error −6.1 ft (`s5_scores.csv`). The 10 largest under-estimates in finding 1 include `77c11e7e…` (ground −2.00, g_inside 5.56, cert 28.1, model 8.7).
- **Why it matters:** `ground_ft` is sold as an observed elevation; −2 ft is a seawall/water value. Every downstream value on those rows (`ffe_ft`, `floor_minus_bfe_ft`, `bfe_call`) inherits it; the population is precisely the VE/AE waterfront.
- **Fix:** in `pipeline/lidar/features.py` mask DEM cells at the hydro-flattened constant and below a low threshold (e.g. < −1.5 ft NAVD88) out of the ring, fall back to `g_inside`/`g_p10` or set `ground_null = not_determinable`; in `assemble.py` null `ffh/ffe` when `ground_ft` is a sentinel. Short term: flag rows with `ground_ft < 0` in the sidecar notes.

### 3. [minor] Handoff §8 counts and the §10 hash do not match the pinned table
- **Evidence** (`claims.csv`): §8 says record 7,482 / modeled 240,305 / null 124,949, conflicts 298, calls above 7,796 / below 33,032 / too_close 35,973 / null 15,442; the table has 7,427 / 240,356 / 124,981, 300, 7,767 / 33,039 / 35,994 / 15,443 (run13 `assemble_12103.json` agrees with the table). §10 quotes the pin as `ecea7173…a6d93b7`; the file's sha256 ends `…d6a93b7` (transposed). The 44.3 % decided share in §8 is 0.442 in the json and table.
- **Fix:** replace §8's numbers with run13's (or label them "run9, superseded by §9") and correct the hash suffix; check `FL_BUILDING_FIRST_FLOOR_ROSTER` in spatia-data was typed from the file, not from §10.

### 4. [minor] Parcel table keys are not unique
- **Evidence** (`s3c_out.txt`): 192 `parcel_key` values appear 2–11 times (465 rows; `FL-12103-15 27 01 00001 777 0000` ×11, distinct `geom_group`); 54 buildings carry such a key; 50 buildings' `parcel_key` row has `buildings = 0`. Per-parcel counts are per condo-unit parcel: `sum(below)` = 884,412 and `sum(buildings)` = 7,137,267 across 432,360 rows (one geometry is shared by 1,104 parcels; 16,571 parcels list > 50 buildings). `any_building_below_bfe` is consistent (0 conflicts).
- **Fix:** dedupe on (`parcel_key`, `geom_group`) or document the multi-polygon parcels; state in `06` that parcel counts are per unit, not per building.

### 5. [minor] `ffe_record_lidar_conflict` semantics drift
- **Evidence** (`s2_s3_out.txt`): the flag is True on 246 record rows, 51 modeled rows and 3 null rows; `06` says "the certificate stays the record value", but the 54 rejected certificates (`record_note` "not used") are modeled or null. Also 269 of the 300 flagged certificates were issued after the 2018-19 lidar and 52 have `year_built ≥ 2019` (`s6_out.txt`): the flag conflates wrong-footprint matches with houses rebuilt after the flight, while `lift_or_rebuild` says `not_evaluated`.
- **Fix:** say in `06` that flagged rows may be modeled (see `record_note`); consider a `rebuilt_after_lidar` hint from `year_built` vs lidar vintage.

### 6. [minor] Record floor heights and lidar ground disagree by > 3 ft on 249 rows without a flag
- **Evidence:** for record rows `ffh_ft` = certificate floor − certificate LAG; `(ffe_ft − ground_ft) − ffh_ft` has median +0.44 (matches the doc) but 249 rows exceed 3 ft (max 13.5); only 43 of the 300 conflict rows are among them. Record `ffh_ft` reaches 23.1 ft (6 rows > 20 ft), `floor_minus_bfe_ft` up to +17.8 (certificates at 24.5–27.8 ft on 2–7 ft ground, calls above).
- **Fix:** add `|cert LAG − ground_ft| > 3 ft` to the conflict screen or note it in `record_note`.

### 7. [note] Band widths
26,638 modeled bands (11.1 %) are wider than 10 ft (6,259 of them unflagged), p95 18.0 ft, max 124 ft; flagged median 10.3 ft vs unflagged 1.92 ft. By construction these resolve to `too_close`, but a 124 ft band on a house is not a usable estimate; cap or null bands above the roof height (`ffh_band_hi > roof_ft` would be the test).

### 8. [note] Record calls use the 2021 FIRM BFE, not the certificate's
For certificates issued before 2021-08-24 the table BFE differs from the certificate BFE by ≥ 2 ft on 1,352 of 4,248 and the side would flip on 496; after the 2021 FIRM 92.2 % agree within 0.5 ft (31 ≥ 2 ft), which validates the static BFE join. Correct choice, but a reader holding the PDF will see a different BFE; say so in `06`.
